DECISIONS (part 3, assumptions the owner can change)
 - Hold on a history row = menu (delete / exclude app) instead of direct delete (the part 2 handoff asked to decide this; menu chosen, delete keeps Undo).
 - Excluding never deletes old entries without asking.
 - Owner's "one improvement of your own": TIMED PAUSE with a picker 15 min / 1 hour / 1 day (auto resume) so a forgotten Pause cannot silently stop the history for days.
 - Pause key is an EXTRA (hidden until turned on), no new icon.
 - All earlier decisions stay (passwords always skipped, own app never, paste never, plain text file, Keystore lock = ASK FIRST, no QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES).
