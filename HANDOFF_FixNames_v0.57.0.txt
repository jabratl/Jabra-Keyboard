DATA: rows = "wrong" glossary sets of the active novel + its My pairs sets (plain text rules only). PREVIEW: same engine (ReplaceRules.run per row; edits = positions). Case-insensitive like the glossary sets. The lock marks are plain case-insensitive finds of the Never change terms.
